Why SkillQuest?

SkillQuest organizes the world's teaching power. We replace traditional courses with a dynamic Credit Economy where every student is a teacher, and every teacher is a student. Join a community that values real-time interaction, peer validation, and continuous learning.

💎 Credit Economy

Earn credits by teaching what you know. Spend them to learn what you don't. A fair, balanced ecosystem for knowledge.

🌍 Live Marketplace

Browse live classes and video courses. Filter by category, difficulty, or outcome to find exactly what you need.

🎥 Live Classroom

Experience real-time HD video classes with screen sharing, interactive tools, and recorded sessions.

🤖 AI-Powered

Automated quiz generation and smart class summaries help you retain knowledge faster and more effectively.

🏆 Reputation System

Build your profile with verified ratings and reviews. Showcase your expertise to the community.

📜 Certification

Earn verifiable certificates upon course completion to demonstrate your mastery to the world.
